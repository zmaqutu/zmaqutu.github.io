01

Two routes to a policy

Value iteration repeatedly updates a grid of values. The Q-learning study combines random exploration with greedy movement toward promising neighboring states. Both record intermediate states for animation.

02

Seeing the reward propagate

The interesting artifact is the sequence rather than just the final route. A positive destination and negative obstacles gradually shape behavior, making discounting and exploration easier to reason about.

03

Read the implementation on its own terms

This historical version uses a scalar value per grid cell in its Q-learning study, rather than a complete state-action table. Its README terminology is broader than the implementation; it is presented here as an early experiment in learning behavior.
